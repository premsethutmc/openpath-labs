# Pump House

`pumps/` — fill a hostel's overhead tank from a sump. Engine: `engines/hydraulics/hydraulics.mjs` (water properties, Darcy–Weisbach + Colebrook, fittings, centrifugal pump from impeller geometry, NPSH, operating point, grade lines).

- **Learn:** seven CE3391 missions — Unit II (Reynolds regime, pipe length, fifth-power diameter law) and Unit IV (shut-valve power, affinity laws, cavitation with hot water, pumps in parallel). Answers are computed by the engine when the learner commits.
- **Explore:** situations (summer water table, hot water, rusted pipes, Ooty altitude, 12-storey tower, morning rush), pump speed and impeller trim, single/parallel/series, valve, pipe diameter/length/material, tank and sump levels, temperature, altitude.
- **Views:** live plant schematic (moving water, gauge pressures, cavitation bubbles), pump-vs-system curves with efficiency, energy and hydraulic grade lines along the pipe, impeller outlet velocity triangle with slip.

Validation: `VALIDATION.md` → Hydraulics.
