# Vision

**Put a world-class industrial R&D lab — like Shell's — in every individual's hands.**

## What we are building

OpenPath Labs is a set of connected simulation engines behind one dashboard. A learner meets a principle inside a real machine, isolates it, owns it, and pushes it to the edge of what is known — in any environment, as many times as they like, at no cost per experiment.

It is not a digitised college lab. A physical lab reproduces a fixed experiment from a manual. We distil each subject down to its purpose — the questions it exists to answer — and let learners apply those principles to anything: a shelf, a bridge, an engine parked in Alaska, an alloy of their own invention.

## Who it is for

- **The student who needs to pass:** a guided path through each subject. Every step is set up; they do one thing, see what happens, and read a short explanation. Enough to understand, not just memorise.
- **The student who wants to go further:** the same world, fully open. Combine parts, invent materials, build a gearbox or an engine, push it to failure, and follow "why?" as far as it goes.
- **Colleges:** a dashboard for every student, with faculty assignment and assessment built on what students *did*, not what they typed.
- **Builders and companies:** the same engines through an API.

## Principles

1. **Meet it in a real machine → isolate it → own it → push it to the edge.** Every concept follows this arc.
2. **Live, not submitted.** Change something and the result updates immediately. No forms, no "run" button where none is needed.
3. **Deterministic physics first, AI at the edge.** The engines give the same, explainable answer every time, at no cost per run. Where a learner goes beyond what an engine models, AI reasons with them — clearly labelled as reasoning, not simulation — and the question tells us what to model next.
4. **Plug and play.** Machines, materials and scenarios (Arctic cold, monsoon wind, an earthquake, four days parked in Alaska) are data fed into engines. Anyone can combine them.
5. **One connected world.** An alloy made in Materials becomes the shaft in a gearbox, loaded by Motion, checked by Solids, heated by Thermo-fluids, driven by a motor from Circuits.
6. **Honest depth.** Every engine states what it models and how confident it is, and is checked against textbook results and published standards. One wrong number destroys trust.
7. **Pause and think.** At the moments that matter, the lab asks a question before it explains.

## Why this is hard to copy

The intelligence is the cheap part. The moat is the integrated engines, the scenario library, the validation against standards, the product experience learners love, the learning data, and relationships with colleges. It takes years of focused building; we start now and stay ahead.
