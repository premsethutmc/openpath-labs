# Mechanical engineering, distilled

Each subject is reduced to the questions it exists to answer. Labs are built around those questions, using real objects.

## Learning path: start to end

| Stage | Questions | Engines | Example objects and scenarios |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Forces and motion around you | What forces act? Does it stay put? | Solids, Motion | Shelf, ladder, crane, bicycle |
| 2. Strength of materials | How hard is the material pushed inside? How much does it bend, stretch, twist? When does it fail? How to make it stronger or lighter? | Solids, Materials | Bracket, bridge, shaft, column, pressure tank, diving board |
| 3. Materials | What is it made of? Why does it fail? Can I make a better one? | Materials, Solids | Alloy blending, heat treatment, cold brittleness, fatigue |
| 4. Making things | How do shapes get made, and what does that do to strength? | Solids, Materials (+ manufacturing models) | Turning, milling, welding, casting, 3D printing |
| 5. Machines | How is motion transformed? | Motion | Linkages, cams, gear trains, steering, quick return |
| 6. Dynamics | What happens when things move fast or shake? | Motion, Solids | Balancing, flywheels, governors, vibration, earthquakes |
| 7. Energy | Where does energy go, and how much is wasted? | Thermo-fluids | Engines, refrigerators, boilers, turbines, pumps, lubrication |
| 8. Heat | How does heat move, and how do we control it? | Thermo-fluids | Fins, insulation, heat exchangers, engine cooling |
| 9. Design | How do I size real parts safely? | All | Bolts, gears, bearings, shafts, clutches, brakes |
| 10. Your project | Can I build a whole working machine? | All | Gearbox, engine, vehicle, robot |

## Strength of Materials — the five questions

1. **What forces act on it?** Free-body diagrams, equilibrium, support reactions.
2. **How hard is the material being pushed inside?** Axial, bending and shear stress.
3. **How much does it change shape?** Strain, deflection, twist.
4. **Where and when will it fail?** Yield, fracture, buckling, factor of safety.
5. **How do I make it stronger — or as strong and lighter?** Section shape, material, supports, load path.

First experience: **Force Playground** — real objects, live loads, live stress, live equations. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
