    About this model

    Bars are 20 mm across and 120 mm between the grips. Loads use the standard strength-of-materials formulas: stress = load ÷ area in pulling, and τ = 16T ÷ πd³ in twisting. Stress peaks at grooves and steps use approximate factors fitted to Peterson's charts, so treat them as close, not exact.

    Ductile metals (steel, aluminium) yield and spread stress peaks, so the smallest cross-section usually decides where they break. Brittle cast iron cannot, so the sharpest stress peak decides. Material values are typical, not for a particular grade.

    Dial readings include a little measurement wobble, like a real rig.
