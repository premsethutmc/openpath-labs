What this model includes

Linear-elastic 2D frame analysis (direct stiffness method): bending, axial force, self-weight, temperature change, scenario loads. Stress σ = N/A ± M·c/I at every point; members in compression are checked for Euler buckling with their full length (pinned ends). Results are checked automatically against textbook formulas.

Not included yet: shear stress, local buckling of thin walls, connection strength, dynamic effects except where a scenario adds them. Material values are typical, not for a specific grade.
