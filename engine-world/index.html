                What is modelled, and what is not

                Reliable: piston and crank motion (exact geometry); oil thickness between 0 and 150 °C (ASTM D341 from each oil's 40 °C and 100 °C data); cooling of a parked engine (Newton's law); the ideal Otto cycle as a check on the cylinder model.

                Approximate: oil thickness below 0 °C (anchored to SAE J300 cold-cranking limits); cylinder pressure (single-zone model, Wiebe burn, fixed wall heat loss); friction (Chen–Flynn, scaled with oil viscosity); cranking speed (starter and cold-battery curves); time to oil pressure (pump and suction-pipe flow); bearing film (Ocvirk short-bearing solution, steady load).

                Assembly choices: bearing clearance changes oil pressure (side leakage ∝ clearance³) and the bearing film; ring gap and oil level change compression and drag by labelled amounts.

                Breathing: valve timing and lift (geometry, reliable); air filling from intake-valve closing, Taylor's inlet Mach index and Helmholtz runner tuning (approximate, Heywood ch. 6); valve float from spring force against valve-train inertia (approximate, cam-ramp factor assumed); valve-to-piston gap from piston position and lift (reliable geometry, assumed pocket depth).

                Spark, fuel and boost: spark timing and mixture enter the cylinder model directly; knock uses the Livengood–Wu integral with the Douaud–Eyzat auto-ignition correlation, calibrated so a 10.5:1 engine on RON 91 at 2000 rpm is knock-limited near 15° (approximate); turbo outlet temperature from the isentropic relation with 72 % compressor efficiency (reliable), spool-up and back-pressure approximate; emissions are textbook trends, not measurements.

                Coolant: freezing point of ethylene-glycol mixes from the standard table (reliable).

                Assumptions: how much old oil thickens; reference engine values (1.2 L, 10.5:1, typical bearings and starter).

                Not modelled: fuel evaporation in extreme cold, combustion chemistry, detailed 3D oil flow, component lifetime. This is a teaching model of the principles, not an engine test.
