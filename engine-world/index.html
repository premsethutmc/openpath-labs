      What is modelled, and what is not

      Reliable: piston and crank motion (exact geometry); oil thickness between 0 and 150 °C (ASTM D341 from each oil's 40 °C and 100 °C data); cooling of a parked engine (Newton's law); the ideal Otto cycle as a check on the cylinder model.

      Approximate: oil thickness below 0 °C (anchored to SAE J300 cold-cranking limits); cylinder pressure (single-zone model, Wiebe burn, fixed wall heat loss); friction (Chen–Flynn, scaled with oil viscosity); cranking speed (starter and cold-battery curves); time to oil pressure (pump and suction-pipe flow); bearing film (Ocvirk short-bearing solution, steady load).

      Assumptions: how much old oil thickens; reference engine values (1.2 L, 10.5:1, typical bearings and starter).

      Not modelled: fuel evaporation in extreme cold, combustion chemistry, detailed 3D oil flow, component lifetime. This is a teaching model of the principles, not an engine test.
