What is modelled, and what is not

Pipes: Darcy–Weisbach with the Colebrook–White friction factor (Moody diagram); laminar 64/Re; Re 2000–4000 is blended and flagged as uncertain. Fitting losses use typical K values (foot valve 5, elbow 0.75, non-return valve 2, gate valve from Crane TP-410).

Pump: a model pump built from its impeller geometry — Euler’s equation with Wiesner slip, friction and incidence losses, leakage, disk friction and bearing losses; NPSH required from the Pfleiderer eye model. It behaves like a typical 2 hp monoblock pump but is not a specific catalogue pump.

Water: density, viscosity and vapour pressure from standard correlations (within 1–2 % of IAPWS tables).

Not modelled: start-up transients and water hammer, air entrainment, pipe ageing beyond the roughness choice, motor characteristics. The cavitation flag is the NPSH criterion; real pumps lose head gradually as NPSH margin shrinks.
