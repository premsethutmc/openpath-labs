What is modelled, and what is not

Pipes: Darcy–Weisbach with the Colebrook–White friction factor (Moody diagram); laminar 64/Re; Re 2000–4000 blended and flagged as uncertain. Fittings use typical K values (foot valve 5, elbow 0.75, non-return valve 2, gate valve from Crane TP-410).

Pump: built from its impeller geometry — Euler’s equation with Wiesner slip, friction and incidence losses, leakage, disk friction and bearing losses; NPSH required by the Pfleiderer eye model. Behaves like a typical 2 hp monoblock pump; not a specific catalogue pump.

Water: density, viscosity and vapour pressure within 1–2 % of IAPWS tables.

Not modelled: start-up transients, water hammer, air entrainment, motor characteristics. Cavitation is flagged by the NPSH criterion; real pumps lose head gradually near it.
