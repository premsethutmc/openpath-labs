What is modelled, and what is not

Model: the textbook reciprocating-pump model — piston in simple harmonic motion (a long connecting rod), rigid water columns (acceleration head ha = (L/g)(A/a)ω²r cos θ), Darcy friction with the friction factor fixed at the mean velocity, air vessels next to the cylinder making the flow beyond them uniform. Separation when the absolute pressure falls below 2.5 m of water.

Checked: the indicator-diagram area matches the power formula P = ρgQ(hs + hd + ⅔hfs + ⅔hfd); air vessels save 84.8 % (single-acting) and 39.2 % (double-acting) of friction work — the standard results.

Not modelled: valve dynamics and leakage timing (slip is a single coefficient), the short pipe between cylinder and air vessel, compressibility, water hammer after separation.
